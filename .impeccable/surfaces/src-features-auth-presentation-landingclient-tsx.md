# Landing page (/)

Scope: public landing page at `/` (`src/features/auth/presentation/LandingClient.tsx`). Visitor mode: Persuade. First surface of a new Pawlio brand world the whole portal adopts over time.

Audience: owners and front-desk staff of pet stores, vet clinics and groomers in Egypt, equally. Action: start signup for their business type, or sign in. Proof: only true facts (paid in app before arrival, every business reviewed before going live, one app for shopping and services, points for pet owners).
