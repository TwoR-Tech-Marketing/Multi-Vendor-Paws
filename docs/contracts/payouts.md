# Contract: payouts

Status: stub. Locked in ticket PAY-1.

## Model (BM-3, D21)

- Every buyer payment goes to TenderPaws.
- Vendors of every type, doctors included, are paid their net on a cycle set per vendor by the admin (weekly or monthly).
- Payouts are manual transfers recorded as payout entries in the earnings ledger.
- Vendors see a statement. Vendors pay nothing upfront (no vendor subscriptions).
